# KISA DESK v1
Eight bounded specialists vote over shared frozen evidence: PRICENET, MICROSTRUCTURE, DERIVATIVES, REGIME, CROSS_VENUE, OPPORTUNITY, SKEPTIC, EVIDENCE_QUALITY.

The desk is SHADOW_ONLY. Consensus failure or a high-confidence skeptic veto forces NO_TRADE. Every desk decision is committed to PostgreSQL evidence and lineage before outcome. No LLM rhetoric has execution authority; agents are deterministic evidence transforms in v1 so their incremental value can be ablated and measured.

Promotion criterion: prospective settlements must beat PriceNet-only and trivial direction baselines after costs, with agent-removal ablations showing positive incremental value.
